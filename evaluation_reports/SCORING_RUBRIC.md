# Candidate Signal Scoring Rubric

## Criteria (0-5 each)
- Mechanistic plausibility
- Regime stability
- Baseline lift persistence
- Calibration and drift resilience
- Failure mode transparency
- Reproducibility

## Rejection rules
Reject or demote when any of the following is observed:
- out-of-regime collapse without explanation
- sensitivity to minor feature perturbations
- dependence on single short window
- no reproducible lift versus naive baseline

## Standard noise verdict language
- "Pattern fails stability checks and is likely noise."
- "Observed edge is not robust across regimes."
- "Hypothesis remains underidentified and unsupported."
